The CE Company has closed

In November 2023, the American Psychological Association did not renew The CE Company's approval to sponsor continuing education. Since then, we have kept this platform running to maintain participant records and support providers as they moved their programs to other sponsors.

With that transition complete, The CE Company has closed. We no longer offer continuing education programs, CE credits or certificates, and this site no longer accepts new accounts, program submissions or certificate requests.

Thank you to the instructors, organizations and professionals we had the privilege of working with.
